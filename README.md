# Personal AI OS

An installable phone app. Free to host. All your data stays on your phone. There is no cloud storage and no account.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages, works from a phone browser)
1. Create a free account at github.com and make a new public repository.
2. Unzip this folder. In the repository tap "Add file" > "Upload files" and upload everything (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, .nojekyll, and the icons and fonts folders).
3. Go to Settings > Pages, choose the main branch and the root folder, and save.
4. After a minute your link appears: https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY/

Cloudflare Pages and Netlify Drop also host this for free.

## Install it
- iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- Android (Chrome): open the link, tap the menu, then Install app (or tap Install under More > Settings).

## Using it
- Turn on the app lock under More > Settings to protect your diary.
- Back up under More > Settings > Storage and backups. "Export everything" includes voice notes.
- To update the app, upload the new files to the same repository and open the app twice.

## Later: a store listing (optional)
The free site pwabuilder.com can wrap the hosted link into an Android package. Publishing on Google Play has a one-time fee.

- The app works offline after your first visit. Updates arrive the next time you open it with a connection.
